# Scientific effects: 2,000-card release, 2026-09-27

The canonical catalog contains 2,000 cards across 19 functional groups, with
linked references for every card. Starting from 1,500, this campaign authored
524 additions in 50 batches and merged 24 overlapping mechanisms, for a net
increase of 500. Target completion describes the catalog, not an exhaustive
survey of science or patents.

## Review and identity

Each accepted addition records its principle, conditions, source-observation
scope, nearby canonical mechanisms and an explicit distinction. Applications,
different materials or renamed variants without a distinct mechanism were
excluded or merged. Merge decisions preserve original source identifiers,
URLs, aliases and legacy card links. Every baseline card remains reachable
through its original identifier or an explicit legacy mapping.

The immutable batch and merge decisions are under `pilot/research/effects`.
`verification-2026-09-27.json` checks 2,000 distinct identifiers, titles and
principle strings, 24 merge decisions, all 50 installed-batch hashes, all 1,500
baseline identities and source pairs, and valid redirect targets. Catalog
SHA-256 (UTF-8 text with LF newlines):
`f057f03f116d56a2d544ce784e50b7d6112e613a28375ea2a6905e0f4571d347`.

Candidate-neighbor review is an editorial judgment, not a proof that every pair
of cards is scientifically inequivalent. Sources were read at the recorded
section, abstract or indexed-excerpt scope; this is not a claim that every paper
was read in full. Disputed microscopic mechanisms and simulation-only results
retain explicit limitations. There are 68 historical topic placeholders, all
accompanied by other actual references; no new card relies only on such a
placeholder. No external LLM API calls were made for this research campaign.

## Patent ingestion and balanced review

At 2026-09-27 02:48:32 UTC, the running corpus reported 11,829,093 documents,
11,828,514 indexed documents and 579 pending. Qdrant reported 11,828,898 points
and green status. These observations are taken while ingestion is active;
SQL and vector counts are not one atomic snapshot. Ingestion is not completed
analysis of those documents.

The previous sequential review remains at 10,851 stored abstract fields read,
2,309 records without abstracts and cursor `AT-512674-A1`. Its cursor was not
advanced by this catalog campaign. The new balanced queue sampled a bounded
2,000-record metadata pool without a CPC/IPC query restriction, then selected
72 publications across A-H and unclassified scheduling buckets. It reviewed
48 technical abstract fields and one literal placeholder; 23 selected records
had no abstract. Fourteen LINK decisions added 15 source links to existing
effects, alongside 24 DESIGN_ONLY and 11 DEFER decisions. These source links
are not additional scientific effects.

The batch spans 28 publication authorities, years 2000-2026 and 68 CPC/IPC
subclasses. All reviewed bucket counts are between four and seven. This improves
the next-review selection breadth without claiming a statistically random sample
or retroactively balancing the previous sequential reviews. Full procedure and
limitations: [balanced review queue](BALANCED_PATENT_REVIEW_QUEUE.md).

## Report changes carried forward

This release retains the deployed mandatory DEEP ARIZ routing, F/G/H method
coverage, always-executed Part 6 advice without a project loop, initially closed
analysis details and mobile report pagination described in
[the workflow release](WORKFLOW_RELEASE_20260927.md).

ARIZ 4.1 now renders the small-people diagram without the lower edge legend
that repeated the complete node paragraphs. The three nodes and two arrows
remain, with short action labels on the arrows instead of numbered references.
Other ARIZ diagrams retain their legends. This rendering change also applies
to existing reports and exports without rerunning analysis. It does not delete
saved ARIZ fields or the expandable detailed record.
